# Lego-Builder (VR & AR)

A brick building game like LEGO.
Build your creations in VR and transfer them to the real-world using an AR headset.

# Details

## VR Building

Enter into a virtual room and start building with unlimited bricks available to you. Use the available options to undo/redo your actions and record them.
Save your virtual recording in a .json file and transfer it to the real world with the AR application.

## AR Replay

Load a .json recording and follow the created step by step guide to build your virtual creation in your living room!

# Installation Details

The VR Unity project is located under the [Lego Builder-VR](./Lego%Builder-VR) folder. Open in Unity and build for your desired headset.

The AR Unity project is located under the [Lego Builder-AR](./Lego%Builder-VR) folder. Open in Unity and build for your desired headset.

Place the VR recording in the StreamingAssets directory of your AR device to make it available for replay.
